📋 Strategy 2 Guidelines

Target Asset Allocation Strategy:

  • Maintain Target Percentages: Each month, check which assets are below their target allocation
  • Buy Underweight Assets: Invest new money primarily in assets that have fallen below target %
  • Disciplined Rebalancing: This forces you to "buy low" by purchasing what has underperformed
  • Risk Management: Prevents individual stocks from growing too large relative to your diversified core

Monthly Process: Input current values → Review recommendations → Invest in underweight assets → Update portfolio next month
